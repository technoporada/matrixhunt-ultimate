// QUICK TIPS

General Gaming Tips

  • Always save often - Use multiple save slots when available
  • Check keybinds - Remap controls to your preference before starting
  • Update drivers - Keep GPU drivers current for best performance
  • Close background apps - Free up RAM and CPU for gaming
  • Use a controller - Many games play better with gamepad input

PC Performance Tips

  • Resolution scaling - Lower resolution for better FPS
  • V-Sync - Enable to prevent screen tearing
  • Frame rate limits - Cap FPS to reduce heat and power usage
  • Game Mode (Windows 10/11) - Enable for priority resource allocation
  • Fullscreen mode - Usually better performance than borderless windowed

Free Games Strategy

  • Epic Games Store - Claim free weekly games every Thursday
  • GOG - Check for periodic free giveaways
  • Steam - Watch for free weekends and trials
  • Prime Gaming - Free games with Amazon Prime subscription
  • Itch.io - Thousands of free indie games and pay-what-you-want bundles
// GAMING GLOSSARY

Common Terms

  • FPS - Frames Per Second (visual smoothness) or First Person Shooter (genre)
  • RNG - Random Number Generation (luck-based mechanics)
  • OP - Overpowered (too strong)
  • AFK - Away From Keyboard
  • PvP - Player vs Player
  • PvE - Player vs Environment
  • MMO - Massively Multiplayer Online
  • RPG - Role Playing Game
  • RTS - Real Time Strategy
  • Soulslike - Difficult action RPGs inspired by Dark Souls
  • Roguelike - Procedurally generated, permadeath games
  • Metroidvania - Exploring interconnected map with ability gating
  • GOTY - Game of the Year edition (includes DLC)
  • AAA - High budget, major studio games
  • Indie - Independently developed games
